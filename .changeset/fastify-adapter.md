Add `@toklyn/fastify` for Fastify 5: `createToklynFastify(auth, options?)` with `requireAuth` and `optionalAuth()` `preHandler` hooks, refresh and logout route handlers, `sendSession`, bearer and cookie mode; `getAuth` and `getOptionalAuth`; `jwksHandler`; and `createToklynFastifyResource(verifier)` for services that only verify tokens.
